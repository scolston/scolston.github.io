I am a PhD Candidate in Atmospheric Science at Iowa State University with a broad interest the weather and climate system, such as earth system modeling, boundary layer meteorology, land-atmosphere interactions and paleo-climatology. Graduate work is largely focused on improving the modeling of land to atmosphere exchanges (surface fluxes) of heat and moisture which are important for cloud and precipitation processes, relevant for both weather and climate prediction. Specifically focused on the impact organized boundary layer circulations, partially resolved in kilometer-scale models, have on surface fluxes. Other work is focused on how land use and land cover changes impact mesoscale convective systems and precipitation extremes.
